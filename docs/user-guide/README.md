# chobii.art User Guide

Welcome to chobii.art - a premium e-commerce platform for posters and photo frames with AI-powered custom artwork generation.

This guide covers everything you need to know to get started based on your role.

## Role Overview

### Customer
Regular users who can:
- Browse and purchase posters and frames
- Create custom AI-generated artwork
- Manage orders and account settings
- Save favorites and track order history

### Trade Member
Professionals (interior designers, architects, staging companies) who get:
- All customer features
- Wholesale pricing (20-30% discount)
- Extended payment terms
- Priority production queue
- Project management tools

### Admin / Super-Admin
Platform administrators who can:
- Manage the complete product catalog
- Process and fulfill orders
- View analytics and reports
- Moderate AI gallery content
- Manage trade program applications

---

## Getting Started

1. **Create an Account**: Visit `/auth/register` to sign up with email or Google
2. **Verify Email**: Check your inbox to verify your account
3. **Explore**: Browse posters at `/posters` or create AI art at `/create`
4. **Purchase**: Add items to cart and checkout with secure payment
